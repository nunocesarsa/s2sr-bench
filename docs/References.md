# References

Back to [Home](Home.md)

## Models

- **SEN2SR**: ESA OpenSR. *A radiometrically and spatially consistent super-resolution framework for Sentinel-2.*
  Code: <https://github.com/ESAOpenSR/SEN2SR> · Weights: <https://huggingface.co/tacofoundation/sen2sr> · Licence CC0-1.0.
- **Satlas super-resolution**: Wolters, Bastani & Kembhavi (2023), *Zooming Out on Zooming In: Advancing
  Super-Resolution for Remote Sensing*, arXiv:2311.18082. Code and weights:
  <https://github.com/allenai/satlas-super-resolution> · Licence Apache-2.0.
- **ESRGAN**: Wang et al. (2018), *ESRGAN: Enhanced Super-Resolution Generative Adversarial Networks*.

## Evaluation

- Wald, Ranchin & Mangolini (1997), *Fusion of satellite images of different spatial resolutions: assessing the
  quality of resulting images*, Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing 63(6).
- Aiazzi et al. (2006), *MTF-tailored multiscale fusion of high-resolution MS and Pan imagery*, PE&RS 72(5):
  MTF-matched degradation and detail injection, the basis of the HPF baseline.
- Wang et al. (2004), *Image quality assessment: from error visibility to structural similarity* (SSIM).
